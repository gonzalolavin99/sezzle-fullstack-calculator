Explain $ARGUMENTS to Gonzalo in Spanish, as preparation for explaining it himself in an English interview.

1. One-sentence summary of what it does and why it exists.
2. Walk through the code in order, pointing to the exact lines.
3. Compare with how he would do it in TypeScript/Node.js or NestJS.
4. Name the trade-off or alternative and why we did not choose it (link the ADR if one exists).
5. Give him a 3–4 sentence version **in simple English** he can say out loud in the interview.
6. Ask one follow-up question an interviewer might ask, and wait for his answer before giving feedback.
